Harbor Smash
Tap to fire. Clear the cargo.

For iPhone and iPad · English

Harbor Smash is a physics puzzle game. You fire a cannon at cargo that is stacked on a dock, a raft or a shipwreck. Your goal in each level is to knock every piece of cargo off the platform.

HOW TO PLAY • Tap the spot where you want the cannonball to land. • A piece is cleared when it falls into the water, lands on the beach, or stops off the platform. Fragile pieces, such as bottles and lanterns, break when the cannonball hits them directly. • Each level gives you a set number of shots and a target number called par. Use par shots or fewer to get 3 stars. Use up to 3 shots more than par to get 2 stars. Otherwise you get 1 star. • The pieces move with physics. Floating rafts tilt and tip over. Some rafts are tied together with rope or drift on the current. Powder barrels explode and can set off other powder barrels.

What is in the game

  • Raft Raid: 500 levels on docks, rafts, a rowboat and shipwrecks, with a boss level every 10 levels.
  • Smugglers' Cave: 20 treasure levels with Slime Ball, Tar Ball and Powder Bomb ammunition.
  • Adventure: Raft Raid and Smugglers' Cave levels in turns.
  • Boosters: Heavy Shot, Broadside and Powder Keg.
  • Daily Bounty, Shipyard, Treasure Map, Treasure Trail and Captain's Chest rewards.
  • Free to play with ads. Optional in-app purchases.

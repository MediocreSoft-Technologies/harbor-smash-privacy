Frequently asked questions

I bought No Ads on another device or after reinstalling. How do I get it back?
Open the game, tap Settings (the gear, top right), then tap RESTORE PURCHASES. You can also tap RESTORE PURCHASES in the Treasury (tap the coin counter). Use the same Apple Account you used to buy it.

Which purchases can be restored?
Only No Ads. Coins, the Starter Pack, Captain's Chest, Treasure Trail islands and Rescue are used up when you receive them, so they cannot be restored.

I paid but did not receive my item.
Close the game completely and open it again; the game delivers unfinished purchases at start. If it still does not arrive, e-mail us with the date of the purchase and the receipt from Apple.

How do I get a refund?
Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Where is my progress saved?
Progress is saved only on your device. If you delete the game, your progress is deleted too. There is no cloud save and no account.

What does RESET PROGRESS do?
It starts the game again from level 1. Your No Ads purchase and your Starter Pack status are kept.

Do I need the internet?
Yes. The game needs an internet connection. Without one, the game pauses until you are online again.

How do I change ad tracking?
Open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad, go to Privacy & Security > Tracking, and switch Harbor Smash on or off. You can play the whole game either way.

I saw an ad that is not appropriate.
E-mail us the time you saw it and, if you can, a screenshot. We will block it.

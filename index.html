Harbor Smash Privacy Policy (iPhone and iPad)

MEDIOCRESOFT TECHNOLOGIES - FZCO

Effective date: 9 November 2026 (2026-11-09)

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This privacy policy explains what information is collected when you play Harbor Smash on iPhone or iPad, who collects it, why it is collected, and what choices you have.

1. Who we are

Harbor Smash ("the game") is published by MEDIOCRESOFT TECHNOLOGIES - FZCO ("we", "us", "our"). We are responsible for the processing of personal information described in this policy.

Contact: support@start2play.games

This policy covers the iOS version of the game (iPhone and iPad). Versions of the game on other platforms use different services and have their own privacy policies.

2. Summary

  • You do not create an account. The game does not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number or postal address.
  • Your game progress is saved only on your device.
  • The game shows ads and uses analytics and crash reporting. Other companies ("partners") provide these services through software kits (SDKs) inside the game. These partners collect information about your device and about how you use the game.
  • Before tracking can happen, iOS asks for your permission. You can say no and still play the whole game.
  • Apple handles all payments. We never see your card or bank details.

3. Information stored only on your device

The game saves one file on your device. It contains your coins, lives, levels played, stars, boosters, settings (music, sound effects, vibration), timers for game features, and whether you bought No Ads or the Starter Pack. This file is not sent to us or to our partners.

The file is deleted when you delete the game. You can also clear your progress with Settings > RESET PROGRESS inside the game. A reset keeps your No Ads purchase and your Starter Pack status.

Your answers to the tracking prompt and to the ad consent form are also stored on your device.

4. Information collected by our partners

When you use the game, partner SDKs can collect the following information:

a) Identifiers

  • Apple's Advertising Identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking (see section 6).
  • Apple's Identifier for Vendors (IDFV).
  • Random IDs created by the SDKs, for example the Firebase app instance ID and IDs created by ad networks.

b) Device and network information

Device model, operating system version, language, time zone, screen size, network type (Wi-Fi or mobile data), mobile carrier and IP address.

c) Approximate location

Country, region or city, worked out from your IP address. The game does not ask for access to your precise location (GPS) and does not collect it.

d) Game usage

Events such as: game launch, level start, shots fired, level win or fail (with shots, stars and time), booster use, offers to continue a level, tutorial steps, screens opened, ads shown, sessions and how long you play.

e) Advertising information

Which ads were requested, shown and tapped, the ad network, the ad format, rewards earned from optional videos, and the revenue from ads.

f) Purchase information

When you buy something, the product ID, price, currency and transaction details that Apple's StoreKit provides. This is used for analytics and ad measurement (see section 5). It never includes your card details.

g) Crash and performance information

Crash reports, error logs, the state of the device at the time of a crash (for example free memory), and performance data such as start-up time.

h) Ad reports

Some ad networks let you report an ad. If you do this, that network receives the information you send in the report.

We do not collect: your name, your e-mail address (unless you e-mail us), phone number, postal address, contacts, photos, videos, microphone or camera data, precise location, health data or payment card data.

5. How the information is used

  • To show ads in the game, including personalised ads if you allowed tracking and, where required, gave consent.
  • To measure ads: to count ad views and taps, to calculate ad revenue, and to find out which ad campaign led to an install of the game (attribution).
  • To prevent fraud and abuse in advertising.
  • To understand how players use the game, so that we can improve levels, difficulty and features (analytics).
  • To find and fix crashes and errors.
  • To change some game settings without an app update, for example how often ads are shown (Firebase Remote Config).
  • To deliver the items you buy and to restore No Ads.
  • To answer your e-mails to support.

We do not sell your personal information for money. Under some laws, for example in California, sharing device identifiers with ad partners for personalised advertising can count as "selling" or "sharing" personal information. You can stop this by choosing "Ask App Not to Track" (section 6) and by contacting us (section 11).

6. App Tracking Transparency (tracking permission)

When the game starts, while the loading screen is shown and before the ad and analytics tools start, iOS shows Apple's tracking prompt. It asks if Harbor Smash may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites.

  • If you choose "Allow", the IDFA is available to the game's partners. They can use it, together with information from other apps and websites, to show you personalised ads and to measure ads.
  • If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the IDFA is not available, and our partners must not track you across other companies' apps and websites. You still see ads, but they may be less relevant to you. You can still play the whole game.

You can change your choice at any time in the Settings app of your iPhone or iPad: Privacy & Security > Tracking, or Settings > Harbor Smash > Allow Tracking.

7. Ad consent in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland

Where the law requires it, the game shows a consent form from Google (Google User Messaging Platform) before ads are requested. In this form you can agree or not agree to the use of your information for personalised ads and related purposes. If you do not agree, the ad partners show non-personalised or limited ads, according to their own rules.

At the moment, the game has no button to open the consent form again. To change your choice, you can e-mail us at support@start2play.games, or you can delete and reinstall the game. After you reinstall the game, the consent form is shown again. Deleting the game also deletes your saved progress.

9. Legal bases (European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland)

  • Consent: personalised ads, tracking, and the use of identifiers for ads and analytics where consent is required.
  • Legitimate interests: crash reporting, security and fraud prevention, analytics to improve the game, and non-personalised ads, which pay for the development of the game.
  • Contract: delivering the items you buy.

10. How long information is kept

  • The save file on your device: until you delete the game or reset your progress.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase: event data linked to an app instance ID is kept for no longer than 14 months.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash data is kept for 90 days.
  • Ad networks and SolarEngine: as described in their own privacy policies.
  • Support e-mails: only as long as we need them to answer your question. Then we delete them.

11. Your rights and choices

  • Tracking: allow or stop tracking in the iOS Settings app (section 6).
  • Ad consent: see section 7.
  • Game data on your device: delete the game, or use RESET PROGRESS in the game's Settings.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, California or other US states), you can have the right to:
    • know what personal information is processed about you, and get a copy of it;
    • correct it;
    • delete it;
    • restrict or object to its processing;
    • withdraw your consent at any time;
    • opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising;
    • not be treated differently because you use these rights.

To use these rights, e-mail support@start2play.games. Because the game has no accounts, we usually cannot connect the information held by our partners to your name. We will help you as far as we can: we will pass deletion requests to Google Firebase where your app instance can be identified, and we will tell you how to contact the ad partners directly. We answer requests within one month.

You also have the right to complain to the data protection authority in your country.

12. Support e-mails

When you tap SUPPORT in the game, your Mail app opens. If you send us an e-mail, we receive your e-mail address, your message, and anything you add to it. We use this only to answer you.

13. Children

Harbor Smash is made for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). If you are a parent or guardian and you think that your child has sent us personal information, for example by e-mail, please contact us and we will delete it.

14. Transfers to other countries

Our partners can process information in countries other than your own, including the United States and Singapore. Where the law requires it, they use safeguards such as the Standard Contractual Clauses of the European Commission.

15. Security

Our partners' SDKs send information over encrypted connections (HTTPS). No method of sending or storing information is completely secure, but we choose partners that protect information with appropriate measures.

16. Links to other websites and apps

The PRIVACY button opens this page in Safari. The SUPPORT button opens your Mail app. Ads can open other websites or the App Store. The privacy policies of those websites and apps apply there.

17. Changes to this policy

We update this policy when the game or its partners change. The effective date at the top shows the current version. Where the law requires it, we will ask for your consent again.
